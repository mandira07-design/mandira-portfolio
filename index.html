Fashion Editorial Poster

Modern Form

PROJECT OVERVIEW

A bold editorial poster concept built around fashion, movement, and modern visual rhythm.

OBJECTIVE

Create a premium fashion-focused composition that feels contemporary while keeping the main message easy to notice.

DESIGN APPROACH

Used strong typography, high-contrast imagery, controlled spacing, and a clean editorial hierarchy to guide the viewer from headline to supporting details.

KEY FOCUS

Typography hierarchy, image placement, contrast, balance, and overall visual direction.

Role: Concept development & graphic design

About the designer

I’m Mandira Das, a graphic designer interested in creating clean, expressive visuals that communicate quickly and feel intentional.

My work explores poster design, promotional graphics, campaign visuals, packaging-inspired compositions, social media creatives, and other visual communication projects.

This portfolio presents selected concept and practice work, with a focus on composition, typography, visual storytelling, and presentation.
